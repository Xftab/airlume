# Help us test Airlume: write 20 questions

Airlume is an app that answers questions about air quality in Pakistan by showing a 3D map. We are testing whether it understands questions written by people who have never seen how it works. **That is why we are not showing you the app or its question format.** Please write the questions you would naturally ask.

## What the app knows
- **Lahore**, with 11 monitoring stations around the city (places like Gulberg, DHA, Shahdara, Wagha Border, Multan Road and others), updated every hour.
- **Punjab's districts** (Multan, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Rawalpindi, Sialkot, Bahawalpur and the rest) and Islamabad, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, at city level.
- Hourly values for the last few weeks, daily values back to 2020, and a forecast for the next 24 hours.
- It reports AQI, PM2.5 and PM10.

## Please write 20 questions
Write them the way you would type them into a chat. Mix of:

- things you would want to know right now;
- things about yesterday, last week, or a particular date;
- comparisons (two places, or now versus before);
- planning questions (when to go out, tomorrow);
- **at least 3 in Roman Urdu** (e.g. "kal Lahore ki hawa kaisi thi?");
- **at least 2 that the app should NOT be able to answer** (anything you like).

Short ones are fine. Do not try to be tricky on purpose, and do not look at anyone else's list.
